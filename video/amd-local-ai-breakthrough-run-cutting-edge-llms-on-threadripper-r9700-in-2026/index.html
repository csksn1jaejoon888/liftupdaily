Limitations and Trade-Offs

While AMD’s stack is impressive, it’s not without constraints. ROCm support (AMD’s CUDA alternative) lags behind NVIDIA’s ecosystem in some areas, particularly for bleeding-edge frameworks like vLLM or TensorRT-LLM. Users may encounter driver quirks when running newer models, requiring manual configuration. Additionally, the 32GB VRAM ceiling—while generous—may limit the largest 100B+ parameter models without aggressive quantization.

Power consumption is another consideration. The Threadripper 9980X and R9700 draw ~600W under load, necessitating robust cooling and a high-wattage PSU. For mobile or edge deployments, AMD’s Ryzen AI 300 series (NPU-equipped) offers a lower-power alternative, albeit with reduced performance.
