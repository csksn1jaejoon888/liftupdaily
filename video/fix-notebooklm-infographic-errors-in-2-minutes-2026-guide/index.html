You’ve spent hours curating sources in NotebookLM, generated an infographic, and—bam—text looks like it was run through a blender. Misspellings, jumbled phrases, and outright gibberish appear the moment you switch to "detailed" mode. (Frustrating, right? You’re not alone.)

What This Trick Lets You Build

  • Error-free infographics: Fix misspellings and garbled text in seconds without manual edits.
  • Consistent branding: Preserve NotebookLM’s visual style while correcting content.
  • Scalable workflow: Process dozens of infographics in batches—ideal for social media or reports.
  • Free-tier friendly: Works on ChatGPT’s free plan, no paid subscription required.

Requirements Before You Start

  • A NotebookLM account (free or paid) with at least one notebook containing sources.
  • The infographic you want to fix (exported as PNG or JPG).
  • A ChatGPT account (free tier works).
  • 5 minutes of focused time—no design skills needed.
  • (Optional) A paid ChatGPT plan if processing high-resolution images frequently.

Complete Infographic Fix Tutorial

Step 1: Generate Your Infographic in NotebookLM

Open your notebook in NotebookLM. Navigate to the Studio panel on the right. Select Infographics from the visualization options. Choose a style (e.g., "NanoBanana" for social media) and set the detail level to "detailed". (This is where errors typically appear.) Click Generate and wait for the output. If the text looks jumbled, proceed to the next step—no need to regenerate.

Step 2: Export the Infographic

Click the download icon (↓) in the top-right corner of the infographic. Save the file as a PNG or JPG to your device. (Pro tip: Use PNG for higher quality if the infographic contains small text or icons.)

Step 3: Upload to ChatGPT

Open ChatGPT in a new tab. Start a new chat. Drag and drop the infographic file into the chat window or click the paperclip icon to upload. Wait for the image to process—this usually takes 5–10 seconds.

Step 4: Use the Magic Prompt

Paste this exact prompt into the chat:

Generate this exact infographic, but with every error in the text corrected. Preserve the original layout, colors, and design. Do not add or remove any elements—only fix spelling, grammar, and jumbled text.

(If you’re feeling adventurous, add: "Explain your corrections afterward." for a breakdown of changes.) Hit Enter and wait for ChatGPT to work its magic.

Step 5: Download and Verify

Once ChatGPT generates the corrected infographic, click the download button below the image. Open the file and compare it side-by-side with the original. (Use a tool like Diffchecker for pixel-perfect comparisons.) If any errors remain, repeat Step 4 with a tweaked prompt:

Fix the remaining errors in this infographic. Focus on the word "[specific error]" in [location, e.g., "the top-left box"].

Troubleshooting: Errors & Fixes

  • Problem: ChatGPT adds extra contrast or alters colors.
    Fix: Add "Match the original color palette exactly." to your prompt. If issues persist, use an image editor like Photopea to adjust brightness manually.
  • Problem: Some text remains jumbled.
    Fix: Zoom in on the problematic section and describe its location in the prompt. Example: "Fix the garbled text in the third bullet point under 'Apollo Missions.'"
  • Problem: ChatGPT refuses to process the image.
    Fix: Reduce the file size (aim for under 5MB) or convert it to a lower-resolution JPG. Free tools like TinyPNG can help.
  • Problem: Corrections introduce new factual errors.
    Fix: Cross-reference the corrected text with your original sources in NotebookLM. If ChatGPT misinterprets data, add context to your prompt: "The term '20 side chains' should not be changed to '30.'"

Where to Take This Further

Now that you’ve mastered the basics, level up your workflow:

  • Batch processing: Use ChatGPT’s Advanced Data Analysis (paid feature) to process multiple infographics at once. Upload a ZIP file and use a prompt like: "Correct all infographics in this ZIP file. Preserve each design and fix errors."
  • Custom templates: Design a reusable prompt template for your team. Include brand-specific terms (e.g., product names) to avoid over-correction. Example: "Never change 'NanoBanana' to 'Nanobanana.'"
  • Automate with APIs: For power users, integrate NotebookLM and ChatGPT via their APIs. Build a script to auto-generate, correct, and export infographics in one click. (Python libraries like requests and Pillow are your friends here.)
  • Community tricks: Join the NotebookLM Community to share prompts and discover edge-case fixes. (For example, some users report better results with "Act as a professional copy editor." prefixed to prompts.)

This trick turns NotebookLM’s infographic feature from a frustrating beta into a reliable power tool. (And hey—if Google fixes the issue in a future update, you’ll already have a bulletproof workflow for other AI-generated visuals.)
