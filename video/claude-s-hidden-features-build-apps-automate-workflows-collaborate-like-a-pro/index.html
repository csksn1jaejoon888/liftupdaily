Claude’s Hidden Features: Build Apps, Automate Workflows & Collaborate Like a Pro

By the end of this guide, you’ll have a working interactive dashboard, a project that remembers your business goals, and a skill that automates tasks across your favorite apps—all built directly in Claude without writing a single line of code.

What This Tool Lets You Build

  • Interactive artifacts: Standalone tools like dashboards, games, or presentation-ready reports that live alongside your chat and update in real time.
  • Persistent projects: AI collaborators that remember your context, goals, and past work—no more copy-pasting or starting from scratch.
  • Automated skills: Integrations with Google Drive, Gmail, Asana, and other tools to pull data, draft emails, or assign tasks without switching apps.

Requirements Before You Start

  • A Claude account (free tier works for all features).
  • Basic familiarity with Claude’s chat interface—no advanced AI knowledge needed.
  • Sample data (e.g., CSV files, meeting notes, or brand assets) to test artifacts and projects.
  • Third-party accounts (e.g., Google, Asana, Notion) if you want to connect skills—permissions can be customized.
  • 5–10 minutes per feature to follow along and experiment.

Complete Claude Tutorial

1. Build Interactive Artifacts

Open Claude and type a clear request: "Create a Facebook ad campaign summary with visuals, key takeaways, and next steps based on this data." Attach your CSV or paste raw numbers. Claude will generate a polished report in a dedicated panel next to your chat.

Refine it instantly: Upload your logo and say, "Use our brand colors." Claude extracts the palette and updates the artifact. No downloads, no scrolling—just natural language edits.

Artifacts go beyond static files: Ask for a tracker, game, or webpage. Each becomes a live workspace you can tweak, share, or revisit later.

2. Set Up Persistent Projects

Click "Projects" in the sidebar and create a new one (e.g., "YouTube Content"). Add your goals, past work, or reference files. Claude automatically saves context, key learnings, and patterns from your conversations.

For example: Start a script in your YouTube project. Claude remembers your style, past videos, and even missteps (like unfinished drafts). Review the "Memory" tab periodically to clean up outdated info—it updates dynamically but may retain old tasks.

Use projects for everything: client work, business strategy, travel planning. Each stays ready to pick up where you left off.

3. Automate with Skills

Click "Skills" in the sidebar and browse integrations. Connect tools you use daily: Google Drive, Gmail, Asana, or Notion. Permissions are customizable—choose read-only or full access.

Try this workflow: Finish a client call, open Claude, and say, "Pull my last Granola meeting notes and draft a follow-up email in Gmail." Claude fetches the notes, composes the email, and lets you review before sending—all without leaving the chat.

Skills vary by tool: Some are read-only (e.g., pulling data), while others let Claude take actions (e.g., assigning Asana tasks). Check the capabilities list when connecting.

Troubleshooting: Errors & Fixes

  • Artifact not updating: Refresh the panel or rephrase your edit request. Ensure your prompt is specific (e.g., "Change the bar chart to a line graph.").
  • Project memory issues: Review the "Memory" tab regularly. Remove outdated info by saying, "Delete the section about [topic]."
  • Skill connection failures: Re-authenticate the tool in "Skills". Check if the integration is read-only or requires additional permissions.
  • Slow artifact generation: Simplify your request or break it into smaller steps (e.g., "First create the outline, then add visuals.").

Where to Take This Further

Combine features for advanced workflows: Use a project to draft a script, invoke a critique skill to refine it, and generate an artifact for the final version. Experiment with chaining skills (e.g., "Pull my Asana tasks, prioritize them, and email the list to my team.").

Explore Claude’s pre-built skill templates for common tasks like email drafting or data analysis. Customize them to fit your needs, or create your own skills for niche workflows.

Share artifacts and projects with teammates to collaborate in real time. Use the "Share" button to generate a link or export files for external use.
