Limitations and Trade-Offs: What CMAX Doesn’t Solve (Yet)

Despite its strengths, CMAX has constraints worth noting:

  • macOS-Only: The native Swift codebase means no Windows or Linux support. Users on other platforms must rely on Ghostty or Tmux, which lack CMAX’s agent integrations.
  • Learning Curve: While simpler than Tmux, CMAX’s dual sidebars and workspaces require memorizing keyboard shortcuts (e.g., ⌘B, ⌘⌥B). The video acknowledges this but argues the trade-off is justified by long-term efficiency gains.
  • Plugin Ecosystem: Unlike iTerm2 or Hyper, CMAX lacks third-party plugins. Advanced users may miss features like shell integration or custom themes, though the open-source nature allows community-driven additions.
  • Resource Usage: GPU acceleration increases VRAM usage by ~150MB compared to CPU-only terminals. Users with older Macs (pre-2018) may experience minor stuttering during heavy agent workloads.
